SDitH (Syndrome Decoding in the Head) is a post-quantum digital signature scheme, currently in NIST's PQC "additional signatures" standardisation process. This repository holds a hardware implementation of SDitH, joint work with Sanjay Deshpande, Jakub Szefer, and Dongze Yue, published as "SDitH in Hardware" at TCHES 2024(2).
