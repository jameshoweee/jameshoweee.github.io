Source code for a forgery attack on CRYSTALS-Dilithium, a lattice-based signature scheme and NIST PQC candidate, exploiting determinism in the signing procedure to mount a practical fault attack against pqm4 implementations. Presented at the 14th ACM ASIA Conference on Computer and Communications Security (ACM ASIACCS 2019), Auckland, New Zealand.
